The Eight Planets

☿️

Mercury

57.9M km

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun. A rocky world with extreme temperatures.

Fastest orbit: 88 days
♀️

Venus

108.2M km

Earth's toxic twin with crushing atmospheric pressure and surface temperatures hot enough to melt lead.

Hottest planet: 462°C
🌍

Earth

149.6M km

Our home. The only known planet to harbor life, with liquid water, a protective atmosphere, and a single moon.

The blue planet
♂️

Mars

227.9M km

The red planet, once flowing with water. Home to the largest volcano and deepest canyon in our solar system.

Two small moons: Phobos & Deimos
♃

Jupiter

778.5M km

The gas giant king. A massive world of storms and incredible magnetosphere with 95 known moons.

Great Red Spot storm
♄

Saturn

1.434B km

The ringed wonder. Famous for its spectacular system of icy rings and 146 known moons including Titan.

Most beautiful rings
♅

Uranus

2.873B km

The ice giant that rotates on its side. A mysterious world of methane clouds and a faint ring system.

Tilted 98° rotation
♆

Neptune

4.495B km

The windy ice giant. The farthest planet with supersonic winds and deep blue coloring from methane atmosphere.

Strongest winds known

About Our Solar System

🌟 The Sun

Contains 99.86% of all mass in our solar system. A 4.6 billion year old G-type main-sequence star.

⚙️ The Goldilocks Zone

Earth sits in the perfect zone where liquid water can exist on a planetary surface - key to life.

🪨 Formation

Formed approximately 4.568 billion years ago from a giant molecular cloud of dust and gas.

🚀 Exploration

Humans have visited the Moon. Robots explore Mars, Venus, Mercury, and the outer planets.
