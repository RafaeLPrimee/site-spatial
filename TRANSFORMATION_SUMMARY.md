## 📊 Technical Specifications

### Rendering Stats
- **Geometry Complexity**: 64-segment icosahedrons (high detail)
- **Material Type**: MeshStandardMaterial (PBR)
- **Lighting System**: 3 PointLights + 1 AmbientLight
- **Shadow Mapping**: PCFShadowShadowMap (realistic shadows)
- **Post-Processing**: UnrealBloomPass (1.8 strength)
